Optional: put texture packs here, each in its own folder (texture_packs/<pack name>/).
Choose one in the Menu: Enhancements > Graphics > Texture Packs.

Texture pack support is experimental: it is still being worked on, and you may run into bugs.

A pack is the art from your own copy of the game, redrawn: run tools/coi-art-dump to dump
it from your ROM, then redraw or upscale the pictures. No game art comes with the port.
